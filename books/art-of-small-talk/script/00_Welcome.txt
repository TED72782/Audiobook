The Art of Small Talk. A master class in twelve lessons.

Welcome.

Before we begin, picture a moment you know well. You are standing in a kitchen at a party, or waiting for a meeting to start, or sitting beside a stranger on a long flight. There is a person right there. And a quiet little voice in your head says: say something. And a second voice answers: say what?

This course is about that second voice. By the end of it, you will always have an answer.

Here is the promise. Small talk is a skill. It is not a personality type, and it is not a gift that some people are born with. It is closer to cooking, or driving. It has a small number of moves. Those moves can be named, practiced, and made your own. People who seem naturally charming are, almost always, people who learned those moves early and have repeated them thousands of times.

We will take it in order. First, why small talk matters, and why most of us fear it more than we should. Then the craft itself: how to open, how to ask, how to listen, how to give something back, and how to keep a conversation moving. Then the advanced work: how to go deeper, how to handle a group, how to leave gracefully, and how to manage the hard cases. We finish with a thirty day practice plan.

Each lesson ends with one small exercise. Please do them. You cannot learn to swim by listening to someone describe water. Ten real conversations will teach you more than ten hours of listening.

One more thing. Throughout the course you will hear short example conversations, spoken by other voices. Listen to the shape of them rather than the exact words. Your words should sound like you.

Let us begin.
